### The finale findings

1. **The completeness cliff is universal (F2 final).** Thinking-model pass@16 under
   its own wrong prefixes: 1.000 / 1.000 / 0.913 at f ≤ .75 — then **0.043 at f = 1.0**.
   Reasoning-RL pushes the cliff edge later (instruct was already at 0.636 by f=.75)
   but does NOT remove it. Once the model's own wrong reasoning is complete, 16
   attempts recover 4% of the time — same as instruct.
2. **RQ2 answered on hard math:** open-block premium for irrelevant traces = +0.364
   (0.375 vs 0.011) for thinking vs +0.012 (0.042 vs 0.030) for instruct. But for
   self_wrong complete traces the premium is +0.021 — continued thinking is only
   used against reasoning the model doesn't recognize as its own conclusion-style.
3. **Audit: seeing ≠ doubting.** After own-complete reasoning, 35% of continuations
   contain "wait" — but 1.2 markers and 1.1k tokens total: token-level doubt, no
   re-examination, 0.02 recovery. After a bare assertion: 100% switch, 75 markers,
   18.7k tokens, 0.71 recovery. The model treats its own completed chains as settled
   even while verbalizing hesitation.
4. RQ3 at f=.25 irrelevant: thinking 0.633 vs instruct 0.085 (matched subsets) —
   the single largest thinking-vs-instruct gap in the pilot.
