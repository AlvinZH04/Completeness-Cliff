### INSTRUCT MODEL INJECTION RESULTS (AIME 24+25, N=16) — completed 09:00

Matched subset (33 qs: solvable at baseline AND has self-wrong traces; baseline pass@1 = 0.587, pass@16 = 1.000 by construction):

| condition | pass@1 | pass@4 | pass@16 | adoption |
|-----------|--------|--------|---------|----------|
| baseline | 0.587 | 0.902 | 1.000 | — |
| self_wrong prefix 25% | 0.536 | 0.781 | 0.909 | 0.155 |
| self_wrong prefix 50% | 0.449 | 0.709 | 0.879 | 0.246 |
| self_wrong prefix 75% | 0.314 | 0.490 | 0.636 | 0.428 |
| self_wrong prefix 100% (open!) | 0.004 | 0.014 | 0.030 | 0.992 |
| self_wrong full_closed | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 0.970 |
| irrelevant prefix 25% | 0.085 | 0.197 | 0.273 | 0.462 |
| irrelevant full_closed | 0.030 | 0.030 | 0.030 | 1.000 |
| wrong_conclusion prefix (open) | 0.511 | 0.814 | 0.970 | 0.398 |
| corrupted prefix 75% | 0.780 | — | — | 0.000 |

**Takeaways (instruct model):**
1. **Total collapse under forced answering**: all full_closed cells → pass@1 ≤ 0.03, adoption 97–100%. The instruct model's answer is fully determined by prefilled reasoning.
2. **Clean dose-response** in prefix fraction, and a **point of no return between f=0.75 and f=1.0**: a complete wrong solution left OPEN (model free to continue) behaves like closed (pass@16 = 0.030 vs 0.000). The instruct model never reconsiders a finished solution — recovery only happens mid-path.
3. **pass@k separates distribution-shift from capability loss**: early wrong prefixes mostly shift the distribution (pass@16 = 0.88–0.91 at f ≤ 0.5) while complete wrong reasoning destroys capability (pass@16 = 0.03).
4. **Irrelevant reasoning is far MORE damaging than wrong-but-relevant reasoning** (pass@1 0.085 vs 0.536 at f=0.25, matched subset) — the model blindly continues the other problem's solution, and 46% of the time literally outputs the donor question's answer.
5. **Corrupted tails of correct traces don't propagate**: pass@1 0.780 at f=0.75 vs 0.587 baseline — the mostly-correct scaffold helps more than corrupted numbers hurt (contrast with Fragile Thoughts' MathError findings).
6. Bare wrong assertions (wrong_conclusion, open) cost little: pass@16 = 0.970.

Caveats: 16384-token budget → 3–18% truncation per open cell (see resolution below); wrong_conclusion:full_closed trunc 90% is benign (forced-box extraction precedes the cap; add a `}` stop string in the next code rev).

### Continuation audit (instruct AIME, 14:40) — `src/audit.py`, report in docs/experiments/audit_instruct_aime.md

Per-sample classification of what the model does AFTER the injected trace (engaged-target = continuation mentions numbers unique to the real question; any-switch = contains "wait/the question asks/different problem/..." phrases):

| cell | correct | adopted | other-wrong | engaged-target | any-switch | mean cont. tokens |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| irrelevant:prefix0.25 | 0.07 | 0.45 | 0.45 | 0.57 | 0.80 | 5820 |
| irrelevant:prefix1 | 0.02 | 0.92 | 0.04 | 0.15 | 0.12 | 1473 |
| self_wrong:prefix0.5 | 0.32 | 0.32 | 0.32 | 0.61 | 0.91 | 5877 |
| self_wrong:prefix1 | 0.00 | 0.99 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.04 | 984 |

**Key insight: noticing ≠ recovering.** At a 25% irrelevant prefix the instruct model usually *does* notice — 80% of continuations contain switch phrases, 57% re-engage the target question's numbers, and it writes 5.8k tokens — yet only 7% end correct: 45% still adopt the donor answer and 45% end at some third wrong answer (tangled between the two problems). After a *complete* trace (prefix1, any source) it doesn't even try: engagement and switch markers collapse, continuations shrink to ~1k tokens, and it just writes the concluding sentence. This extends Yang et al.'s identification-vs-recovery gap with token-level evidence, for an instruct model.

**Truncation caveat RESOLVED (10:45)**: 32k rerun gives statistically identical pass@k (pass@1 0.550 vs 0.548; pass@16 0.783 vs 0.783) with truncation 11.5% vs 12.4%. Doubling the budget doesn't move either number → the ~12% truncation is the instruct model's **intrinsic non-termination rate** (repetition loops on unsolvable problems), not a budget artifact. The 16k instruct numbers stand as-is. Methodological note: a high truncation rate should trigger a budget-doubling check, and only counts against validity if pass@k moves.

**Takeaways (thinking model, maze):**
1. **RQ2 answered on maze: recovery is thinking-driven.** wrong_conclusion open vs closed = 0.939 vs 0.077 — a gigantic open/closed gap the instruct model completely lacked. Given room to think, the model re-examines a bare wrong assertion (100% switch phrases, ~63 correction markers, 8.5k re-thinking tokens) and solves the problem itself.
2. **Recovery scales with how much re-thinking gets triggered**: irrelevant 25% prefix → 13k-token continuations, 82% correct; irrelevant complete → 2.3k-token continuations, 38% correct; own complete wrong reasoning → 376-token continuations, 2% correct.
3. **The blind spot survives reasoning-RL: the model does not distrust its own completed reasoning.** self_wrong:prefix1 ≈ full_closed (0.023 vs 0.008, adoption ~0.98) — same point-of-no-return as instruct (n=8 caveat; AIME grid will give the robust version). A *bare* wrong conclusion is rejected (0.939) but the *same wrongness with plausible supporting steps* is accepted — the reasoning scaffold, not the conclusion, is what persuades.
4. corrupted → 0.994+: numeric corruption fully repaired mid-trace.
5. Thinking ≫ instruct at every comparable cell (e.g. irrelevant f=0.25: 0.819 vs 0.085) — but the instruct comparison is cross-task here; the AIME grid gives the matched comparison.
