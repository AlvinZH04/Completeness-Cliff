Thinking model, maze baseline 0.941. Compare against same-dataset irrelevant (another maze's trace):

| cell | same-dataset (maze donor) | cross-domain (AIME donor) |

**Domain mismatch triggers the re-examination that same-domain irrelevance suppresses.** With the think block open, a complete *math* trace inside a *maze* question is escaped 78% of the time (audit: 87% of continuations contain switch phrases, 22 correction markers/sample, 7.3k re-thinking tokens), while a complete *other-maze* trace captures the model (0.384, adoption 0.80, only 14% switch phrases). The more alien the injected reasoning, the more likely the model is to notice and restart. Recovery is gated on a *mismatch-detection* signal, not on reasoning ability per se.

**Grading artifact caught:** same-dataset maze `irrelevant:full_closed` pass@1 = 0.200 with adoption = 1.00 — every sample parroted the donor answer, and maze answers (small step counts) collide with gold ~20% of the time by chance. Cross-domain AIME donors (integers 0–999) almost never collide → the TRUE forced-answer floor is ~0.03. Same-dataset maze irrelevant numbers are collision-inflated; prefer cross-domain (or collision-filtered) numbers for maze. AIME targets don't have this issue (donor collisions ≈ 0).

Audit: `docs/experiments/audit_thinking_maze_cross.md`. The reverse direction (AIME questions × maze-trace donors — the flagship "does an obviously-alien trace free the model on hard problems" test) auto-started on GPU1.
